# X draft queue

Drafts for the account named in `voice.md`. Nothing here has been posted — the founder posts
by hand, every time.

**This is a publishing pipeline, not idea state.** It is cross-idea by nature, which is why
it lives here and not under `reports/`. Per-idea findings stay in their idea's
folder; each entry below carries a `source:` pointer back to the artifact it came from
instead of restating it.

## Entry format

Append newest at the bottom. IDs are `X-{NNN}`, never reused.

```
### X-001 · YYYY-MM-DD · <angle>
status: <vocab:x_post_status>
angle: <vocab:x_post_angle>
disclosure: <vocab:x_disclosure_tier>
source: <path#anchor, "session", or "founder-raw">
media: <content/x/media/ path — media posts only; founder attaches by hand>
reply_to: <URL of the post being replied to — replies only>
voice: confirmed | provisional
posted_url: <filled in by the founder after posting>

**A**
<draft text>

**B**
<draft text>

**Grounding**
| claim | source | grade |
|---|---|---|
| <the checkable assertion> | <file#anchor or "public: URL"> | <vocab:evidence_quality or "public"> |

**Notes**
<why this framing; what was deliberately left out; any open permission question>
```

Every variant of one idea shares an entry — they are the same claim wearing different
clothes, and splitting them would lose the comparison the founder is making when they pick.

## What must never go in

Contact names, verbatim quotes from private threads, screenshots, or any claim above the
disclosure tier recorded on the entry. `references/disclosure.md` in the skill is the
authority; when in doubt the answer is to anonymize down a tier, not to ask forgiveness.

Do not maintain a count, a status summary, or a "currently drafted" line anywhere in this
file — it would drift from the entries beneath it within a week. Read the entries.
