Six gates make the plan convincing

Each gate is a reason to gather specific evidence. Passing a later gate never excuses skipping an earlier one.

01

Measurement works in real water

Blind held-out polymers, calibrated concentration, inline flow, bubbles, fouling and explicit unknown rejection.

Do not sell until thresholds are agreed and met.
02

The three-month path is resourced

Week-by-week dependencies for labelled data, parts, reference tests, pilot water and site access.

Do not treat a target date as evidence.
03

A brand will pay

Named programme census, real budget owners, prototype reviews and price tests at three levels.

Do not call 20-50 brands a market before this.
04

Matter terms create an asset

Cost reimbursement, site consent, synchronized data, model rights and named commercial access.

Do not fund bespoke partner hardware without terms.
05

One mill loop saves money

Choose rinse, dye, treatment, aeration or backwash. Run a controlled intervention and hold quality.

Do not promise broad resource savings before this.
06

The $20B ceiling survives bottom-up math

Deduplicated sites, spend by line, influenceable share, adoption, price, competition and gross margin.

Do not present the ceiling as TAM before this.
