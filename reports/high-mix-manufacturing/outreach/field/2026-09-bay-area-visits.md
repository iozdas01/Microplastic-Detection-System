## What to ask, by stop type

Ask about the past, never about the idea. No pitch at any stop.

**Reuse yards and liquidators** (1, 3, 4, 7, Building Resources)

- "Of the furniture that comes in, roughly how much is solid wood or metal frame versus the pressed-board stuff?" → **H1A9**, the root. Anyone who unloads a truck knows this instantly.
- "Do you charge to take it away, or do you pay for it?" and "what does it cost you to dump what you cannot sell?" → **H1A10**. Both numbers, same person.
- "What comes in that you turn away, and why?"
- "Where does the good stuff go? Who buys it and what do they pay?"

**Restoration shops** (2, Renaissance)

- "Walk me through the last piece you did. How many hours, split by step?" → **H1A12**.
- "What did it sell for, and what would the same piece have gone for unrestored?" → **H1A11**.
- "What comes in that you refuse?"

**Cabinet and millwork shops** (5, 6)

- "When a job comes in that is different from the last one, what actually breaks?" → the belief.
- "How long does it take you to give someone a firm price on a one-off, and what makes it take that long?" → **H2A3**.
- "Where does the drawing come from, and how much retyping happens between the drawing and the machine?" → the belief, stated as their workflow.
