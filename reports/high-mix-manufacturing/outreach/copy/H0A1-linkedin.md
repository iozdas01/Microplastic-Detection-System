## The follow-ups, once any of them answers

Do not fire as a list. Each unlocks only if the previous got a real answer.

1. **"Whose was it, in the end?"** — the design, the drawing, or the floor.
2. **"What did it cost to put right?"** — let them compute.
3. **"Out of the last hundred, roughly how many?"** — the H0A1 number. Ask it last and as a
   range: Leonard's answer was 1 in 50, and people who will not produce a percentage will
   happily correct a wrong one.
4. **Only after a real story: "has anyone ever been put on checking drawings before release,
   and what happened?"** — that is H0A2, and asking it early gets a policy instead of a history.
