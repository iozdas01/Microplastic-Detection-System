What the map is for

Three layers of the same trade, four miles apart, all walkable in a day: a 1934 independent on 14th Street, a designer's workroom on Natoma, and a national's appointment-only suite on California Street. Each carries the same failure on a completely different P&L.

Two gaps are visible rather than hidden. Art Shade Shop's layer is a guess — whether they fabricate in-house or only sell and fit decides layer 1 versus layer 2, and it is answerable by walking in. And no franchise network, big box or manufacturer has been reached at all, which is layers 3, 5 and 7 empty.
