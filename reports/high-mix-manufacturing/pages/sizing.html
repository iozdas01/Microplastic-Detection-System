What this model cannot tell you

  • Whether anyone will buy. The sale price is an input, not a finding. Restoration Hardware marks reclaimed pine up 4× ($45 → $180/board foot) and Apple keeps 85% of new price on refurbished — so a premium is achievable. It is not automatic.
  • Whether supply aggregates. Kaiyo raised ~$50m to do only the collect-and-resell half and wound down in 2024 on logistics cost.
  • Whether the recoverable share is real. Most discarded furniture is MDF or particleboard, which cannot be recycled into new board. The 15% default is a guess and it is the single biggest swing in the model.
